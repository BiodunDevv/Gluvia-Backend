## 📋 PORTION CONTROL RULES

### Swallows (Choose ONE per meal)

- ✅ Best: Wheat Swallow (small wrap)
- ✅ Good: Oat Swallow (small wrap)
- ✅ Good: Unripe Plantain (small portion)
- ⚠️ Limit: Amala (small wrap only)
- ⚠️ Limit: Pounded Yam (small wrap)
- 🚫 Avoid: Eba (or very small)

### Rice (Max 1 cup cooked)

- ✅ Best: Brown Rice, Ofada Rice
- ⚠️ Limit: Jollof Rice (small plate)
- 🚫 Caution: White Rice (very small)

### Protein (Eat plenty!)

- No limits on grilled/boiled protein
- Choose fish, chicken, eggs, beans
- Avoid fried when possible

### Soups (Eat freely)

- All Nigerian soups are safe
- High in vegetables and protein
- Very low GI
